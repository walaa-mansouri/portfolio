Frequently asked questions

How long does it take?

It depends on the pages and features. Your quote includes the expected schedule.

Are hosting and domain included?

External subscriptions and provider fees are separate unless expressly included in your quote.

Can I add extra features?

Yes. Booking, multilingual content, administration and integrations can be quoted.

Does SEO guarantee customers?

No. I build technical SEO foundations and relevant local SEO and GEO structure, without promising rankings or customers.

What do I need to provide?

A short description of your business, contact details, logo if available, photos and service information. We can prioritise missing content together.

Will it work on mobile?

Yes. The design is built for phones, tablets and desktops, with checks of key user journeys.

Will I keep my domain and access?

Yes, accounts can remain in your name. Ownership of deliverables and third-party licences are agreed in the quote.

How will my website help generate enquiries?

Clear messaging, trust signals, simple navigation and visible calls to action make contacting you easier, without guaranteeing lead volume.

Le Cryptosystème RSA

Fondements Mathématiques et Analyse de Sécurité

EncadranteMireille Fouquet
RecommandationJean-Yves Ducloux
ÉcoleUniversité Paris Cité · 2025–2026

This project studies the RSA cryptosystem, one of the most influential public-key algorithms in computing history - from the mathematical foundations (modular arithmetic, Euler's theorem, the Chinese remainder theorem) to key generation, encryption and digital signatures, and a full security analysis covering factorization attacks, the common modulus attack, blinding, and the Bleichenbacher attack on PKCS#1 padding that directly compromised SSL/TLS.

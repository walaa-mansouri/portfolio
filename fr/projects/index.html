Le Cryptosystème RSA

Fondements Mathématiques et Analyse de Sécurité

EncadranteMireille Fouquet
RecommandationJean-Yves Ducloux
ÉcoleUniversité Paris Cité · 2025–2026

This project studies the RSA cryptosystem, one of the most influential public-key algorithms in computing history - from the mathematical foundations (modular arithmetic, Euler's theorem, the Chinese remainder theorem) to key generation, encryption and digital signatures, and a full security analysis covering factorization attacks, the common modulus attack, blinding, and the Bleichenbacher attack on PKCS#1 padding that directly compromised SSL/TLS.
